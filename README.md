**## Sales Analytics Case Study## **

An end-to-end Business Intelligence project that analyzes retail sales data using SQL, Databricks, Power BI, and Looker Studio to generate business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

### Project Objectives

- Calculate Daily Selling Price per Unit
- Determine Average Selling Price
- Analyze Gross Profit and Gross Profit %
- Evaluate promotional effectiveness using Price Elasticity of Demand (PED)
- Identify sales trends and profitability patterns
- Present findings through interactive dashboards

### Key Insights

- Sales fluctuate throughout the period.
- Average selling price remained relatively stable.
- Sales and Cost of Sales closely follow each other.
- Promotions increased quantity sold.
- All three promotion periods showed **elastic demand**, meaning customers responded positively to price reductions.
- Weekend and Quarter 2 sales performed better than other periods.

### Recommendations

- Continue strategic promotional campaigns.
- Improve gross profit by reducing Cost of Sales.
- Increase stock and marketing during peak sales periods.
- Monitor pricing to balance sales growth with profitability.
- Use dashboards to support continuous business decision-making.
